Examplol for adults Other brands: Fakeprofen, Notarin

  • About examplol
  • Who can take it

Examplol is a pretend painkiller used only in tests. It helps with mild pain & swelling (inflammation).

You can buy it from pharmacies.

How to take examplol

The usual dose is:

  • 1 or 2 tablets, up to 3 times a day
  • at least 4 hours between doses

Take the smallest dose that works, e.g. 1 tablet, for the shortest time.

Who can and cannot take examplol

Breastfeeding and examplol

If you’re breastfeeding, check with a pharmacist first. Very little gets into breast milk (under 1%).

Pregnancy and examplol

Pretend options include:

  • pretend option two
